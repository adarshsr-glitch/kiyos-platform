## Plain text for TTS

Families with money in India and the US rarely know where to start with their taxes. Kiyos changes that. Meet Arjun. Like most of our customers, he starts on WhatsApp.

Kiyos asks a few quick questions: his move, his years abroad, his accounts and his family. He just taps. Within minutes he gets a first assessment of what needs attention in India, in the US, and where the two overlap, with a PDF copy to keep.

General questions get an answer that cites the law. Anything about his own situation goes to a qualified expert.

So he chooses a plan, priced for his household, with a named expert who owns his case. He confirms the scope, pays, and his workspace opens.

Here's Arjun a few weeks in. First, what his expert needs from him. Then his year in both countries: his residency status and his income by source. Every figure opens the document behind it. And Kiyos never calculates tax itself: tax figures and ways to save come from his expert, with their name on them.

He can see his whole year on one timeline, and every document he sends is read and checked against what he's told us.

Messages is his WhatsApp thread, inside the workspace. One tap sends a question to his expert, Vikram. Let's switch sides.

Vikram's view opens on exactly that question. Kiyos has already drafted an answer from Arjun's record. Vikram reviews it, edits it if needed, and sends it. The reply reaches Arjun's workspace and his WhatsApp.

Vikram works from the same picture Arjun sees: residency in both countries, a timeline of moves and deadlines, and income. When he spots a way to save, he picks from options set in law and adds his own reasoning. The system suggests; the expert decides.

Back on Arjun's side, Vikram's reply is waiting at the top of his overview, with a badge on Messages.

That's one customer. Vikram's home shows all of his customers: work due week by week, where documents are stuck, and what needs an expert now.

Finally, how we keep the AI honest. A fixed set of questions runs against the live agent. When one fails, it shows why, with a suggested fix. Apply it, the question runs again, and it passes. Every change to the agent is visible and can be undone.

That's Kiyos. Customers start on WhatsApp, experts get the full picture, and the AI works inside rules we can test and improve. Thank you.

## How it was cut

There are 25 segments, taken in order from the combined video. Several are sped up (1.1× to 3×) where the action is just taps or scrolling. The closing frame is slowed to 0.75× to hold it. The edit list is in the session scratchpad (`edl.txt`, `cut.swift`).
